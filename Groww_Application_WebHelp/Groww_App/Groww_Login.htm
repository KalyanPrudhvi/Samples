Groww Login

Groww App is very simple to use and the interface is quite easy to understand. For account opening it will take 2-3 working days. Follow the below steps to open an account in the GROWW app.

  • Enter the app with the sign in option by giving mail ID.
  • And then verify the mobile number with OTP.
  • Create a password for the account to login and Security Pin to open app.

  • Upload Documents as per the instructions to open a DMAT account for Trade transactions.

NOTE - Enter Security Pin whenever you want to open groww app.
