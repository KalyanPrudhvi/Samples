Installing Application

The Application of Groww is suitable for both Android and IOS users. App is available in Playstore. Follow the below steps to install Groww in your device.

  • Go to Google Play Store.
  • Search Groww in the search bar.
  • Find stock trading, Dmat account- Groww app.
  • Click Install and give permissions to access some data.
