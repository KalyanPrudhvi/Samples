Account details

Account details is the place where all self information be available.

  • Tap Profile picture, On the main interface.
  • Click on your Account Details.
  • Tap your name, Click Basic Details.

  • you will redirect to the page where all the information available.
  • Click on Edit to change Mobile number and Email (If required).
  • Find your account Unique Client Code, Demat Acc Number/BOID, DP ID. Copy them if required.
  • Tap Trading preference to enable NSE or BSE at your interest of Stock Exchange.
  • Tap Nominee Details to see the details of Nominee details.
  • Tap Account Related Forms, to download Account Opening Form and Segment Form.
