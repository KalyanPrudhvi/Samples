Reports

For any Office, Salary, and tax redemption purpose you can download the official statements/documents of the your trade account.

  • Click Profile Picture at left top corner.

  • Tap your Name, Click Reports>.

 

  • Tap profile picture.
  • Go to Reports at bottom.
  • Tap Tax Proff-80C ELSS Statement and click download to get statement for the current financial year.
  • Repeat the process for to get Capital Gains- Mutual Funds, Capital Gains-Stocks, Groww Ledger, P&L Report, Contract Note, and Demat Report.
