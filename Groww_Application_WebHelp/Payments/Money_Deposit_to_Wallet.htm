Money Deposit to Wallet

Money Transactions in Groww App is to be done by Groww Wallet. It is virtual wallet dedicated to account login credentials. All Transactions should gone through the Wallet.

  • Tap profile picture at right hand side top corner.
  • Click Your Investments.

  • Tap +ADD MONEY at left side.

  • Tap +ADD MONEY at right side first row to add money.

  • Enter the amount to be added into wallet, and
  • Tap > to select payment methods.

  • And then, Tap ADD MONEY at bottom, It will redirect to payment application for approval.
  • After successful transaction, It will redirect to GROWW application as Payment Successful.
  • Tap DONE to complete the process.
