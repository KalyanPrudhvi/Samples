Money Withdraw from Wallet

After a successful trading 80% of money credited into wallet on the day itself and the remaining 20% will be credited into wallet on the next day. We can withdraw the money from wallet to the bank which we give details at the time of account opening.

  • Tap on Icon .
  • Select WITHDRAW, Enter the amount you want draw from Groww wallet.
  • Check the bank account linked, change if required.
  • Tap WITHDRAW MONEY, Done money will be credited to your linked Bank account.

  • Tap Profile icon at right top corner.

  • Tap GROWW Balance at first row under the amount.
  • Tap Withdraw > to draw the wallet money to the linked bank account.
  • Enter amount that you want to withdraw.
  • Select Withdraw > to select the banks that you details.

  • Tap Withdraw at bottom to credit money in the selected bank.
  • Get Withdraw processed. Tap DONE to complete the withdraw transaction.
