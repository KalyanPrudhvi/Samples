Buy Orders

Before selecting a stock, do a proper research of individual choice on the Company fundamentals:-

  • Market Capital,
  • P/E ratio, and,

  • Dividend Yield.

After deciding on what stock you want to buy, Follow the below steps to buy the order:-

  • Find the company in the search bar that you want to buy.
  • Click Company stock.
  • Tap Buy at bottom.

  • Enter the number of Stocks in the First Field Box that you want to buy.
  • Click Drop down arrow at Type to select Delivery at  Intratrade.
  • Select NSE or BSE in Drop down list at Qty.
  • Select Market Price or Limit Price in Dropdown list
  • Tap BUY at bottom to buy the stock.
