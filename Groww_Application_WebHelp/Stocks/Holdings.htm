Dashboard (Portfolio)

When you successfully bought stocks, your stocks will appear in portfolio.

  • Switch to Dashboard in your main interface.
  • Go to Your Investments in main interface.
  • Monitor your stock holdings on daily basis for the price movements.
  • Find the invested amount, Current value and Total Returns along with 1Day returns.
  • Sort out stocks based on COMPANY, MKT PRICE, RETURNS (%), and CURRENT. Tap on them individually.

  • Tap Vertical bar signal to sort out your stocks based on current value, Return%, Day change %, Company (A-Z), and clear checkbox based on your preference.
