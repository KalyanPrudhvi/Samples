Sell Orders

Once you bought a stocks that will create a portfolio in dashboard. Analyze your Holdings day to day for further improvements.

Follow the steps to sell the stocks in your portfolio:

  • Switch to dashboard in your main interface.
  • Tap Your Investments in main interface.
  • Select stock that you want to sell.
  • Tap SELL on the new tab at bottom.

  • Enter the number of stocks to sell in First Field Box at left side.

  • Click Dropdown arrow at TYPE to select Delivery for Intratrade.
  • Select NSE or BSE in Drop down list at Qty.
  • Select Market PRice or Limit Price in Drop down list.
  • Tap SELL at bottom to sell the stock to the respective BSE or NSE indices.
  • Enter the TPIN sent to your mobile.

Note - Request TPIN everyday by entering BOID and OTP at TPIN portal.

  • Get your stock sold successful, Tap DONE to return to main screen.
  • Money will be credited into your wallet.
